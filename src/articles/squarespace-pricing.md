Squarespace pricing in 2026 comes down to three plans: Basic at $12/month, Core at $17/month and Advanced at $25/month when you pay for a year up front, or $19, $27 and $36 if you pay month to month. The sticker price is only part of the bill, though. Transaction fees, card processing, the domain after year one and the billing cycle you pick all change what you actually pay.

Everything below comes from Squarespace's own pricing page and help center, checked on **October 27, 2026**. We didn't buy a Squarespace plan to test it, so you'll find published facts and the math behind them here, not invented benchmarks.

> **Disclosure:** Youmake is an alternative to Squarespace. We've stuck to Squarespace's own published numbers, including where it's the better fit.

## Squarespace plans at a glance

| Plan | Paid annually | Annual total | Paid monthly | Online store transaction fee | Digital content and memberships fee |
|------|---------------|--------------|--------------|------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Basic | $12/mo | $144/yr | $19/mo | 2% | 7% |
| Core | $17/mo | $204/yr | $27/mo | 0% | 5% |
| Advanced | $25/mo | $300/yr | $36/mo | 0% | 0% |

Prices are in US dollars. Squarespace says paying annually saves "up to 36%." There's no free plan. Every site starts with a 14-day free trial, and the pricing page says no credit card is required for it.

## How much does Squarespace cost per year?

The gap between monthly and annual billing is bigger than it looks on a per-month basis. Over twelve months:

| Plan | 12 months on monthly billing | 12 months on annual billing | Difference |
|------|------------------------------|-----------------------------|------------|
| Basic | $228 | $144 | $84 |
| Core | $324 | $204 | $120 |
| Advanced | $432 | $300 | $132 |

Annual billing also brings the free domain (below), so if you know you'll keep the site for a year, it's the cheaper route by a wide margin. The trade-off is commitment: Squarespace's refund policy gives a full refund on annual plans canceled within 14 days of purchase. After that, annual plans aren't refunded, and monthly plans are never refundable. If you got a free domain with the plan, its cost is deducted from that refund.

Switching works in one direction faster than the other. Moving from monthly to annual takes effect right away, with a prorated credit for the unused part of the month. Moving from annual to monthly waits until the current cycle ends.

## What each Squarespace plan includes

All three plans share the core site builder: templates and the drag-and-drop editor, built-in AI tools, mobile-optimized sites, SEO tools, SSL, unlimited bandwidth, 24/7 support, forms, newsletter blocks, unlimited products and services, and checkout on your own domain. The differences show up in a few places:

| Feature | Basic | Core | Advanced |
|---------|-------|------|----------|
| Contributors | 2 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| AI credits | 10 (one-time) | 20/month | 120/month |
| Video hosting | 30 min | 5 hours | Unlimited |
| CSS/JavaScript customization and code injection | No | Yes | Yes |
| Advanced website analytics | No | Yes | Yes |
| Promotional pop-ups, Mailchimp, Zapier | No | Yes | Yes |
| Shipping labels, carrier-calculated shipping | No | No | Yes |
| Discounts, payment plans, Google Shopping | No | No | Yes |

**Basic** is a site with two contributors and a light store. The 2% store fee and 7% fee on digital content make it a poor deal once you sell much.

**Core** is the plan Squarespace recommends. It drops the store transaction fee to 0%, opens up custom code, and gives unlimited contributors.

**Advanced** is for real e-commerce: the lowest card rate, no fee on digital content, and shipping and discount tools.

## Squarespace transaction fees and payment processing

There are two separate charges when you sell, and they're easy to mix up.

**1. Squarespace's own transaction fee**, set by your plan: 2% of store sales on Basic and 0% on Core and Advanced. For digital content and memberships (courses, video pages, member sites) it's 7% on Basic, 5% on Core and 0% on Advanced.

**2. Payment processing**, charged on every card payment. Squarespace Payments rates in the US, per its help center:

| | Basic | Core | Advanced |
|--|-------|------|----------|
| Standard cards | 2.9% + $0.30 | 2.9% + $0.30 | 2.5% + $0.30 |
| Premium cards (American Express and select business cards) | 3.2% + $0.30 | 3.2% + $0.30 | 3.2% + $0.30 |
| International cards | +1.5% | +1.5% | +1.5% |
| Currency conversion | +1% | +1% | +1% |
| ACH direct debit | 1.5% | 1% | 1% |

If you use Stripe or PayPal instead, their own processing rates apply. Squarespace also lists an automated tax fee of 0.15% per transaction on Core and 0.05% on Advanced, each capped at $0.99.

A worked example: $1,000 of physical-product sales on Basic, paid with standard US cards, costs $20 in Squarespace transaction fees on top of 2.9% processing plus $0.30 per order. On Core, the $20 goes away and you pay processing only. That's why Core usually costs less than Basic for anyone with a working store.

The help center also lists a **Plus** plan (2.7% + $0.30 card rate, 1% on digital products) that Squarespace says is rolling out in waves and may not appear on the pricing page in your country. It wasn't on the US pricing page when we checked.

## The free domain and other extra costs

- **Domain:** annual plans include one year of free domain registration on eligible domains. You have to register it within a year of starting the subscription, it's limited to one per site, and it renews at Squarespace's standard rate plus taxes after the first year. Monthly plans don't get the free domain.
- **Older plans:** Squarespace no longer sells its earlier Personal, Professional and Premium plans on the pricing page. If you're on one of those or another older plan, your price and fees can differ from the tables above; the help center says to check the Billing panel for your actual price.

## Squarespace review: who it fits

Squarespace is a polished template builder. Going by what it publishes, it fits well if you:

- want a good-looking site from a template and are happy to edit it by dragging blocks;
- run a small store, a portfolio or a booking-style business and want hosting, domain, payments and email marketing in one bill;
- don't need to take the site's code with you.

It fits less well if you want the site built *for* you from a description, need a web app with logins and data, want a mobile app or a pitch deck from the same project, or need the source code in your own GitHub. ## FAQ

**How much does Squarespace cost?**
Billed annually: Basic $12/month ($144/year), Core $17/month ($204/year) and Advanced $25/month ($300/year). Billed monthly: $19, $27 and $36. Card processing and, on Basic and Core, some transaction fees come on top.

**Does Squarespace have a free plan?**
No. Squarespace offers a 14-day free trial, and its pricing page says no credit card is required to start it.

**Does Squarespace charge transaction fees?**
Basic charges 2% on store sales and 7% on digital content and memberships. Core charges 0% on store sales and 5% on digital content. Advanced charges 0% on both. Payment processing fees (2.9% + $0.30 per standard US card on Basic and Core, 2.5% + $0.30 on Advanced) apply on every plan.

**Is the Squarespace domain free?**
For the first year, on an annual plan. After that it renews at Squarespace's standard rate plus taxes. Monthly plans don't include it.

**Can I get a refund from Squarespace?**
Annual plans canceled within 14 days of purchase get a full refund, minus the cost of any free domain. Monthly plans and annual plans canceled later aren't refunded.
