Lovable and Bolt both turn a prompt into a working web app, and both start paid plans at $25 a month. The difference is how they charge: Lovable counts credits per action, Bolt counts tokens. That choice decides which one feels cheap and which one surprises you at the end of the month.

## Lovable vs Bolt at a glance

| | Lovable | Bolt |
|--|---------|------|

## How the pricing models differ

Lovable gives you a daily allowance of build credits on every plan, then adds monthly credits on Pro and Business. Its docs say monthly plan credits expire two months after issue, and top-up credits last twelve months. Workspaces can have unlimited members on all plans, because pricing follows credits rather than seats.

Bolt sells a monthly token pool. Free gives you 1M tokens a month with a 300K daily cap. Pro gives 10M tokens with rollover and no daily limit. Teams starts at 10M per member, also with rollover.

A credit maps to one unit of work you can see. A token maps to how much text the model reads and writes, which grows with project size. A small edit in a large Bolt project can use more tokens than the same edit in a small one. That is hard to predict until you have watched your own usage for a week.

## Free plans: what you really get

Lovable's free plan is built for trying it out. You get 5 build credits a day, capped at 30 a month, and you can invite up to 10 people to a published project. You cannot edit or download code, attach a custom domain or remove the Lovable badge.

Bolt's free plan is larger in raw usage. It includes public and private projects, unlimited databases and website hosting. Sites carry Bolt branding, and the plan excludes custom domains, connectors and MCPs, and the newest AI models. Bolt also notes that the free plan needs no card.

If you only want to see whether the idea works, either is fine. If you want something you can hand to a customer, both free plans stop short of that. Custom domains start on the paid tiers.

## Which one fits which builder

**Lovable** suits people who want predictable per-action costs and a Git workflow from day one. GitHub sync is on every plan, which matters if a developer will take over later. See our [Lovable pricing breakdown](/articles/lovable-pricing) for every credit tier.

**Bolt** suits people who work in bursts. Unused tokens roll over, and Pro removes the daily cap, so a long weekend of building does not hit a wall on day one. Teams adds admin controls, billing and shared templates.

Neither pricing page tells you what a finished client project will cost. That depends on how many changes you ask for, which is why a one-week test with your own prompt beats any comparison table.

## FAQ

### Is Lovable or Bolt cheaper?

At the entry paid tier they cost the same, $25 a month. Which one costs less over a month depends on your usage pattern. Lovable charges per credit, and Bolt charges per token with rollover, so heavy bursts favor Bolt's model and small steady edits favor Lovable's.

### Can I use Lovable or Bolt for free?

Yes, both have free plans. Lovable gives 5 build credits a day, up to 30 a month. Bolt gives 1M tokens a month with a 300K daily limit. Neither free plan includes a custom domain. For a bigger comparison see [free AI website builders](/articles/free-ai-website-builder).

### Can I keep my code if I leave?

Lovable's docs say code download is included from Pro and Git sync works on all plans. Bolt's pricing page does not say. For a builder-by-builder answer, see [AI app builders with code export](/articles/ai-app-builder-code-export).
