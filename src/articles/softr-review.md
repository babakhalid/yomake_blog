In this Softr review, the short version is that it's a good fit for teams who want a secure portal over existing data with little setup. It's a weaker fit if you need more than a handful of outside users on a cheap plan, the source code, or anything beyond a web app.

## What is Softr?

Softr's docs call a client or partner portal that shares Airtable data with external users "one of the most common usages of Softr," and it now has its own database too. Softr's docs recommend Softr Databases if you're starting from scratch, and say Softr "connects natively to over 20 different data sources" if your data lives elsewhere.

You assemble an app from blocks (lists, tables, kanban boards, calendars, charts, forms), set user groups and permissions, and publish to a Softr subdomain or your own domain. Softr also includes Workflows for automation and a REST API and MCP server for working with your apps and databases from other tools.

## Softr pricing (October 2026)

Softr's pricing page opens on yearly billing, which it labels "2 months off." The prices below are the ones shown with yearly billing selected. Monthly billing costs more; check the monthly price on the toggle before you pay. Prices don't include tax.

| Plan | Price (billed yearly) | AI credits / mo | Builders | App users (Team + Client) | Database records | Workflow actions / mo |
|------|----------------------|-----------------|----------|---------------------------|------------------|-----------------------|
| Free | $0 | 5 | Up to 3 | 5 | 5K | 500 |
| Basic | $19/mo | 10 | 1 included, extra $5/mo | 5 + 5 | 50K | 2.5K |
| Pro | $99/mo | 50 | 2 included, extra $10/mo | 10 + 50 | 500K | 10K |
| Business | $329/mo | 100 | 3 included, extra $20/mo | 30 + 100 | 1M | 25K |
| Enterprise | Contact sales | Custom | Custom | Custom | Custom | Custom |

What changes by plan, from the pricing page:

- **Free:** unlimited apps, databases, workflows, agents and forms within the limits above, plus the Softr API and MCP.
- **Basic:** custom domain, conditional form logic, automated database backup and extra built-in workflow actions.
- **Pro:** remove Softr branding, one-time code and Google sign-in for app users, granular permissions, comments, custom code and API connectors. You can also buy extra app users, starting at $1/month.
- **Business:** two-factor authentication, domain-restricted signup, global data restrictions, an installable mobile app (PWA), push notifications, and priority support with onboarding. Extra app users start at $2/month.
- **Enterprise:** SSO (SAML, OpenID), SCIM, audit logs, custom agreements and SLAs.

Other pricing details worth knowing:

- **Extra custom domains** cost $15/month, or $13/month billed yearly.
- **Free trial:** everyone gets one at signup, with no credit card required, and you can extend it once by trialing a specific plan.
- **Refunds:** in full within 14 days of your first monthly payment, or 30 days of your first yearly payment.
- **Discount:** 50% for non-profits and education.

### The detail that matters for client portals

Softr splits app users into two kinds. **Team users** share a business email domain with a builder in your workspace. **Client users** are everyone else, and Softr counts all free email domains, such as Gmail, as Client users. Basic includes 5 Client users, and you can only add app users beyond the allowance on Pro and above. If you're building a portal for outside clients, plan on Pro at $99/month billed yearly, which is also the plan that removes Softr branding.

## Softr's AI features

Softr now calls itself an AI app builder for business operations. Its docs describe four AI features:

- **AI Co-Builder.** From the dashboard you choose "Generate with AI" and describe the app. The docs say it may ask 2-3 clarifying questions, then creates the database tables and relationships, pages and navigation, user groups with permissions, and a theme. You can keep asking it for changes inside the editor.
- **Vibe Coding Block.** For anything the standard blocks can't do, this block generates a custom UI component from a prompt and can connect it to live data. Softr isolates the generated code to that one block, so it can't break the rest of the app.
- **Softr Agents.** AI assistants that chat with your app users or run on a schedule, with tools to read and write records, send emails or Slack messages, and search a knowledge base you upload.
- **Database AI Agents.** These fill in and enrich fields inside a Softr Database table.

Two honest notes from the docs. First, the AI Co-Builder can create simple workflows, but Softr says complex multi-step automations "may need manual adjustments," and conditional form branching is set up by hand. Second, AI credits are tight on the lower plans: 5 a month on Free and 10 on Basic. Softr says credit use depends on the request, with a full app from scratch costing more than a single block. Plan credits reset monthly and don't roll over, though add-on credits roll over for one month. The upside is that you can keep building in the no-code editor after your credits run out.

## What Softr does well

- **Fast portals over data you already have.** If your clients, projects and invoices already live in Airtable or HubSpot, Softr puts a login-protected interface on top without moving the data.
- **Permissions are built in.** User groups, page visibility and global data restrictions (on Business) are core features, not add-ons.
- **A real free plan.** You can test the builder and publish a small app without paying.
- **A safety net for AI code.** Keeping generated code inside one Vibe Coding Block is a sensible design for non-technical teams.

## Softr's limits

- **Client users get expensive.** The Team vs Client split means a portal for outside clients outgrows Basic's 5 Client users almost at once, and branding removal waits until Pro.
- **Your data source sets the ceiling.** Softr's own comparison table puts Airtable at roughly 200-300 recommended concurrent users and Google Sheets at 50-100. For heavy traffic, Softr recommends Softr Databases, SQL, Supabase or Xano.
- **No source code to take with you.** The docs describe exporting data (CSV or XLSX) and PDFs of pages, and the Softr CLI covers workflows and databases. We found nothing on exporting an app's source code to run elsewhere.
- **Web apps first.** A mobile experience comes as a PWA on the Business plan. Softr doesn't build slide decks.
- **Workflows pause at the limit.** When you hit your monthly workflow actions, Softr pauses workflows until the limit renews or you upgrade.

## Is Softr good?

Yes, for a specific job: an internal tool or a portal on top of Airtable, HubSpot or Softr Databases, built by an operations person who wants permissions handled for them and doesn't need the code. It's less good for agencies billing many outside clients, founders who want to own their code, or anyone who needs a website, an app and a pitch deck from one place.

## FAQ

**How much does Softr cost?**
Softr has a free plan. Paid plans billed yearly are Basic at $19/month, Pro at $99/month and Business at $329/month. Enterprise is priced by sales. Monthly billing costs more, and prices exclude tax.

**Is Softr free?**
Yes. The free plan is "free forever," with 5 AI credits a month, up to 3 builders, 5 app users, 5K database records and 500 workflow actions a month. A custom domain needs Basic or higher.

**Can you export your code from Softr?**
Softr's docs describe exporting data as CSV or XLSX and pages as PDF. We found no option to export an app's source code. If owning the code matters, look at a builder with code export or GitHub sync, such as Youmake.
