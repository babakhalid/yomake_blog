## What a free vibe coding tier actually limits

Free tiers differ less in price than in the kind of limit they use. There are four:

- **Daily caps.** You get a small number of requests per day and nothing more until tomorrow.
- **Monthly credits.** A fixed pool you can spend in one afternoon.
- **Project or app caps.** A ceiling on how many apps you can keep.
- **Locked features.** Custom domain, code export or GitHub sync reserved for paid plans.

A daily cap suits someone who builds ten minutes at a time. A monthly pool suits a burst. Neither suits a client deadline.

## Why "free" stops working at launch

Free tiers are built for trying, and a launch needs four things they tend to hold back:

1. **Your own domain.** A client or customer will not trust a builder subdomain for long.
2. **The code.** If the tool disappears or you outgrow it, you need to take the project with you.
3. **Headroom.** A real site takes dozens of small changes. A 7-message day or a 25-credit month ends that fast.
4. **A clean hand-off.** If you build for clients, they need access without sharing your account.

That is why a free tier is best used as a test of the tool's output, not as a place to run a business.
