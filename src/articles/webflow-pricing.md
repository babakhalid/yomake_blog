Webflow pricing has two layers. A **Site plan** pays for one website: hosting, a custom domain, pages, CMS items and bandwidth. A **Workspace plan** pays for the place where you and your team build: staging sites, seats, collaboration and code export. To publish a site on your own domain, you need a paid Site plan ($15/month billed yearly for Basic). Code export needs a paid Workspace plan on top ($19/month billed yearly for Core).

## Site plans vs Workspace plans: what's the difference?

Webflow's help center puts it this way: "Workspace plans operate at the Workspace level, meaning their features affect all of the sites in your Workspace," while "Site plans, on the other hand, work at the individual site level."

In practice:

- **You need a Site plan** for every site you want to publish on a domain you own (yourbusiness.com). One site, one Site plan.
- **You need a paid Workspace plan** if you want more than two free webflow.io staging sites, team collaboration tools, or code export.
- **You may need both.** An agency that hosts client sites on Webflow and also wants export or advanced roles pays for a Workspace plan plus one Site plan per client site.

Every Webflow account starts with a free Starter Workspace and free Starter sites, so you can design for as long as you like before you pay. The bill starts when you publish to a custom domain or bring in a team.

## Webflow Site plans (pricing per site)

|------|---------------|----------------|--------------|
| **Starter** | Free | Free | webflow.io domain, 2 static pages, limited CMS (50 items), 1 GB bandwidth, 50 form submissions |
| **Basic** | $15/mo | $25/mo | Custom domain, 300 static pages, 10 GB bandwidth, unlimited form submissions, password protection. No CMS |
| **Premium** | $25/mo | $39/mo | Everything in Basic, plus the Webflow CMS (20,000 CMS items, 40 Collections), 50 GB bandwidth, code components, site search, form file upload |
| **Team** (Platform plan) | $2,500/mo | Annual contract required | Everything in Premium, plus Webflow Localize, AEO agents, publishing workflows, 5 full seats and 5 limited seats included |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Custom | Custom roles, granular permissions, dedicated account manager, enhanced SLAs |

A few details worth knowing before you pick:

- **Basic has no CMS.** If you plan to run a blog, a portfolio with many projects or any list of content you'll keep adding to, you need Premium.
- **Premium bandwidth scales up.** The base Premium plan includes 50 GB. You can step up to 2.5 TB, and each step costs more: for example +50 GB adds $20/month billed yearly ($30 monthly).
- **Webflow Cloud is included.** Site plans can host Webflow Cloud apps next to the site (5 apps on Starter and Basic, 15 on Premium), with usage-based charges past the included limits.

Webflow sells two families of Workspace plans: one for in-house teams and one for freelancers and agencies.

More AI credits come in packs of 2,000 credits a month for $20/month billed yearly ($25 monthly).

## How much does Webflow cost in practice?

Because the two layers stack, the real number depends on what you're doing. Using Webflow's listed prices:

- **One small business site, no blog, you build it yourself:** Basic Site plan, $15/month billed yearly ($25 monthly). The free Starter Workspace is enough.
- **One site with a blog or CMS:** Premium Site plan, $25/month billed yearly ($39 monthly).
- **A freelancer who wants code export for client hand-off:** Freelancer Workspace at $16/month billed yearly, plus a Site plan for each client site you host on Webflow.
- **A small team of three designers on one CMS site:** Premium ($25) plus two extra full seats ($39 each), so $103/month billed yearly before any Workspace upgrade.

Two billing rules from the pricing page affect all of these: prices are "per site, plus applicable taxes," and if you cancel a plan "you'll still pay the remainder of the term." Paying yearly saves up to 40%, but it locks you in for the year.

## Which Webflow plan includes code export?

Code export is on paid Workspace plans only: Core, Growth, Freelancer and Agency. Webflow's help center is direct about it: "Site plans do not include the ability to export your site's code."

What you get is a ZIP file with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and images. What you don't get matters more if you plan to hand a site over or move it. According to Webflow's help center, exported code leaves out:

- CMS content (Collection lists show their empty state)
- User Accounts and Ecommerce content and functionality
- Site and page password protection
- Form submission processing, reCAPTCHA and site search
- Localized content (only the primary locale is exported)
- Code components

You can export CMS Collections separately as CSV, and the pricing page notes that "if you export your code, it can't be reimported." So Webflow export works as a static snapshot or a backup. It won't turn the site into a project a developer keeps building on. ## Who Webflow is a good fit for

Webflow suits people who want pixel-level visual control and are happy to learn a designer tool to get it:

- **Web designers and design-led agencies** who build marketing sites by hand and want classes, interactions and a CMS without writing front-end code.
- **Marketing teams** that publish lots of pages and blog posts and want editors to update content without touching the design.
- **Larger teams** that need staging, roles, publishing permissions and localization, which Webflow sells through Growth, Agency, Team and Enterprise.

Webflow also has AI features now. Its pricing page lists "Build a site: Turn a prompt into a fully customized, production-ready site," plus AI-generated sections, copy and code components, all running on a monthly AI credit allowance.

## Where Webflow is a weaker fit

- **You want to describe the project and have it built.** Webflow's AI can draft a site, but the product is built around the visual Designer, and that's where the editing happens.
- **You need an app, not a website.** Webflow builds websites and stores. It doesn't make native mobile apps or slide decks, and logic-heavy web apps live in the separate Webflow Cloud product.
- **You want to own a working codebase.** Export needs a paid Workspace plan, leaves out CMS, forms and ecommerce, and can't be reimported.
- **You want one simple bill.** Site plans, Workspace plans, seats, AI credits and add-ons such as Analyze (from $9/month) and Localize (from $9/month) are priced separately.

## FAQ

**How much does Webflow cost?**
Webflow has a free Starter plan on a webflow.io domain. To publish on your own domain, Site plans start at $15/month billed yearly (Basic, $25 monthly). Premium, which adds the CMS, is $25/month billed yearly ($39 monthly). Ecommerce plans start at $29/month billed yearly. Paid Workspace plans, needed for code export and team features, start at $16/month (Freelancer) or $19/month (Core) billed yearly.

**What's the difference between Webflow Site plans and Workspace plans?**
A Site plan covers one website: custom domain, hosting, pages, CMS and bandwidth. A Workspace plan covers how you build: staging sites, seats, collaboration and code export, across every site in the Workspace.

**Can you export code from Webflow?**
Yes, on paid Workspace plans (Core, Growth, Freelancer, Agency), not on Site plans. The export includes HTML, CSS, JavaScript and images, but not CMS content, forms processing, ecommerce, user accounts or site search, and exported code can't be reimported.

**Is Webflow worth it?**
For designers and agencies who want hand-tuned visual control and a strong CMS, yes. If you'd rather describe what you want, need a web app, mobile app or slides, or want the full codebase in GitHub, a Webflow alternative such as Youmake fits better.
