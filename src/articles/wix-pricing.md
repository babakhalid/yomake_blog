## Wix pricing for every plan (October 2026)

These are the USD prices on wix.com/plans. Wix says the displayed prices are for yearly subscriptions, paid in full at the time of purchase, and that prices and currency vary by location.

| Plan | Price (billed yearly) | Storage | Site collaborators | Payments and ecommerce |
|------|----------------------|---------|--------------------|------------------------|
| Free | $0 | Not listed | Not listed | No |
| Light | $17/mo | 2 GB | 2 | No |
| Core | $29/mo | 50 GB | 5 | Accept payments, basic eCommerce, scheduling and services |
| Business | $39/mo | 100 GB | 10 | Accept payments, standard eCommerce, scheduling and services |
| Business Elite | $159/mo | Unlimited | 100 | Accept payments, advanced eCommerce, scheduling and services, advanced developer platform |
| Enterprise | Custom | - | - | Tailored; Wix says pricing "varies based on project scope" |

What each step up adds, according to the plan cards:

- **Free:** hosting, the drag-and-drop editor, no-code design features and Wix's templates. Your site lives on a wixsite.com address, and Wix's FAQ says a Premium plan is what lets you connect your own domain and remove Wix branding.
- **Light:** a free domain for one year, 2 GB of storage, multi-cloud hosting, a "light" marketing suite and 2 site collaborators. No online payments.
- **Core:** the first plan that accepts payments, with basic eCommerce, scheduling and services, 50 GB of storage and 5 collaborators.
- **Business:** marked "Recommended" by Wix. Standard eCommerce, a standard marketing suite, 100 GB and 10 collaborators.
- **Business Elite:** advanced eCommerce and marketing, the advanced developer platform, unlimited storage and 100 collaborators.

Every paid plan lists a custom domain, hosting, AI creation tools, 24/7 customer care and the 14-day money-back guarantee.

## How much does Wix cost per year?

Because the listed prices are monthly rates on a yearly plan paid up front, the first charge is the full year:

| Plan | Monthly rate | Paid up front for 12 months (before tax) |
|------|-------------|------------------------------------------|
| Light | $17 | $204 |
| Core | $29 | $348 |
| Business | $39 | $468 |
| Business Elite | $159 | $1,908 |

Wix notes that "additional subscription periods are available", but monthly-billing prices aren't shown on the pricing page. If you don't want to commit for a year, check the final price on the purchase page before you pay. Taxes are added on top, based on your billing address.

## The costs that aren't in the plan price

**Payment processing.** If you sell through Wix Payments in the US, Wix's help center lists these fees:

| Payment method (US) | Fee per transaction |
|---------------------|--------------------|
| Credit/debit card (excl. American Express) | 2.9% + $0.30 |
| American Express | 3.7% + $0.30 |
| PayPal | 3.49% + $0.49 |

Wix also lists a 1.5% cross-border fee, a 1% currency conversion fee when the payment currency differs from your site's, and a $15 chargeback fee in the US ($20 for PayPal).

**The domain after year one.** The free domain voucher is only for the first purchase of a Premium plan, must be redeemed within 60 days, and only covers a new domain bought from Wix. It doesn't cover renewing a domain or a plan upgrade, so from year two you pay for the domain separately.

**Email.** A personalized address (info@yourdomain.com) comes through a separate Google Workspace plan, according to Wix's FAQ.

## Which Wix plan fits which person?

- **Trying Wix out:** Free. Fine for a draft or a hobby page, as long as the wixsite.com address and Wix branding are acceptable.
- **A simple site on your own domain, no selling:** Light at $17/month yearly.
- **Taking bookings or selling a few products:** Core at $29/month yearly is the cheapest plan with payments.
- **A growing store or service business:** Business at $39/month yearly, with more storage and collaborators.
- **Large catalogs or teams that need developer tools:** Business Elite at $159/month yearly, or Enterprise.

## Wix review: where it's strong and where it isn't

Wix is a mature platform. Bookings, stores, marketing tools and more than 2,000 templates sit in one dashboard, and the AI website builder can start a site from a single prompt. For a local business that wants everything under one login, that's a good deal.

The trade-off is lock-in. Wix's help center says a Wix site runs only on Wix's servers because "the SaaS architecture does not support external hosting." You can move your domain, but not the site's code. If you stop paying, you rebuild somewhere else. Wix is also built around websites and stores. For a custom web app, Wix bought [Base44](/articles/base44-review), which is a separate product with its own pricing.

## FAQ

**How much does Wix cost?**
Wix has a Free plan. Paid plans billed yearly are Light at $17/month, Core at $29, Business at $39 and Business Elite at $159, paid up front for the year. Taxes, payment processing fees and domain renewal after the first year cost extra.

**Which Wix plan do I need to sell online?**
Core ($29/month billed yearly) is the cheapest plan that accepts payments and includes eCommerce. Light and Free don't.

**Does Wix include a free domain?**
Yes, for one year, with the first purchase of a yearly or multi-year Premium plan. You redeem it within 60 days on a new domain from Wix. Renewals aren't covered.

**Can I cancel Wix and get a refund?**
Wix offers a 14-day money-back guarantee on the initial upgrade to a Premium plan.

**Can I export my Wix site?**
No. Wix says sites must run on its servers and can't be hosted elsewhere. If you want code you can take with you, look at builders with code export, such as Youmake.
