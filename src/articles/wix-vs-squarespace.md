## Wix vs Squarespace pricing, plan by plan

The two price lists don't line up neatly, so here are the plans matched by what they unlock. Squarespace's pricing page shows both billing options. Wix's blog lists US prices for yearly billing; its plans page says prices and currency vary by location, so check the figure at checkout. For the full breakdowns, see our [Wix pricing guide](/articles/wix-pricing) and [Squarespace pricing guide](/articles/squarespace-pricing).

| Tier | Wix plan (billed yearly) | Squarespace plan (yearly / monthly) |
|------|--------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Entry | Light, $17/mo | Basic, $12/mo / $19/mo |
| Mid | Core, $29/mo | Core, $17/mo / $27/mo |
| Upper | Business, $39/mo | Advanced, $25/mo / $36/mo |
| Top | Business Elite, $159/mo | No equivalent listed (Squarespace offers no plan above Advanced on this page) |

What changes by plan:

- **Wix Light** gives you 2 GB of storage, a free domain for one year and 2 site collaborators, but no payments or ecommerce.
- **Wix Core** adds payments, "Basic eCommerce," scheduling, 50 GB of storage and 5 collaborators. **Business** moves to "Standard eCommerce," 100 GB and 10 collaborators. **Business Elite** adds "Advanced eCommerce," unlimited storage, an "Advanced developer platform" and 100 collaborators.
- **Squarespace Basic** includes templates, the drag-and-drop editor, up to 2 contributors, 30 minutes of video and unlimited product and service sales. **Core** adds unlimited contributors, advanced analytics, code injection and 5 hours of video. **Advanced** adds unlimited video, discounts, payment plans, carrier-calculated shipping and commerce APIs.
- **Domains:** both include one year of free domain registration (on Squarespace, with annual plans).

On price alone, Squarespace is cheaper at every matching tier. Wix's advantage is the free plan, which lets you build and publish before you pay anything.

## Design and templates

Wix says it offers "2000+ website templates," all free to start from. Squarespace doesn't publish a template count on its templates page; it leans on fewer, design-led starting points plus its AI builder.

One detail worth knowing before you start on Wix: its help center says that "If you created a site with AI, you cannot change it to a different template." The workaround is to create a new site and move your Premium plan to it. So if you're unsure about the look, decide before you generate.

As a general pattern, Squarespace sites tend to look consistent because the editor keeps you inside a structured layout, while Wix gives you more freedom to place elements, which also makes it easier to end up with a messy page.

## Ease of use

Both are no-code, drag-and-drop editors, and both now let you start with AI instead of a blank template.

- **Wix** starts you in a chat. In the Wix Harmony Editor, Aria asks questions about your business, fills in a site profile, then generates pages, layouts, colors, starter content and images that you keep editing by hand or by asking Aria.
- **Squarespace** starts you with questions too. Its blog says you "answer a few questions about your business, and it generates a multi-page website" with starter copy, images and a visual style you can revise.

If you've never built a site, both will get you to a first draft quickly. The difference shows up later: Wix gives you more apps and settings to manage, and Squarespace gives you fewer choices to make.

## Ecommerce: Wix or Squarespace for selling?

| | Wix | Squarespace |
|--|-----|-------------|
| **Plan you need to sell** | Core ($29/mo yearly) or higher | Any plan, from Basic |
| **Platform transaction fee** | Not mentioned on the pricing page | Basic: 2% on store sales, 7% on digital content and memberships. Core: 0% and 5%. Advanced: 0% and 0% |
| **Card processing (US)** | Wix Payments: 2.9% + 0.30 USD per card transaction (excl. American Express) | Rates not listed on the pricing page |
| **Automated tax** | Not stated on the pricing page | Not on Basic; 0.15% (max $0.99) per transaction on Core; 0.05% (max $0.99) on Advanced |

If you sell a few products, Squarespace Core at $17/month billed yearly removes the store transaction fee for a lower price than Wix Core. If you sell digital content or memberships, only Squarespace Advanced drops that fee to 0%. Wix's case is breadth: its higher plans add more ecommerce tiers and scheduling for service businesses.

## SEO tools

Both cover the basics a small business needs. Neither will rank a site on its own; the content does that.

- **Wix** lists a personalized SEO setup checklist (with keyword research through Semrush), editable meta tags and canonical tags, an automatic XML sitemap, structured data on products, events and blog posts, an editable robots.txt, a 301 redirect manager with bulk import of "up to 500 URLs at once," and Google Search Console reports inside the dashboard.
- **Squarespace's** SEO checklist covers SEO titles and descriptions per page, URL slugs, alt text, redirects, a custom 404 page, an SEO report that flags missing alt text and descriptions, a search keywords panel, and verification with Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.

Wix gives you more technical controls (robots.txt, structured data, bulk redirects). Squarespace keeps the list shorter and easier to follow.

## Wix or Squarespace: which should you pick?

- **Pick Squarespace** if you want the lower monthly price, a clean design without much fiddling, and the ability to sell from the entry plan.
- **Pick Wix** if you want to start on a free plan, need bookings and scheduling, want the larger template library, or want deeper SEO settings.
- **Pick neither** if you need more than a website: a client portal, a booking app, a mobile app, or a pitch deck for the same business. Or if you want the code in your own GitHub.

## FAQ

**Is Wix or Squarespace cheaper?**
Squarespace, at every matching tier. Billed yearly, Squarespace runs $12, $17 and $25 per month; Wix runs $17, $29, $39 and $159. Wix does have a free plan, on a Wix-branded URL with ads.

**Is Squarespace better than Wix for ecommerce?**
For a small store on a budget, often yes: you can sell from Squarespace Basic, and Core ($17/month yearly) has no store transaction fee. Wix needs Core ($29/month yearly) to take payments, and adds more ecommerce tools on higher plans.

**Which is better for SEO, Wix or Squarespace?**
Both cover titles, descriptions, redirects and Google Search Console. Wix offers more technical controls, such as an editable robots.txt, structured data and bulk redirects.

**Can I export my site from Wix or Squarespace?**
Neither pricing page lists code export. If owning the code matters, use a builder that exports it, such as Youmake.
