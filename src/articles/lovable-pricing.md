Lovable pricing starts at $0 on the Free plan, then $25 a month for Pro and $50 a month for Business, each with 100 monthly credits at the entry tier. Both paid plans scale up to 10,000 credits a month. Everything you do in Lovable, from building to hosting to the AI features inside your app, draws from that one credit balance, so the real cost depends on how much you change and how many people use what you ship.

We make [Youmake](https://www.youmake.dev/), an AI app builder for websites, web apps, mobile apps and slide decks, so we read competitor pricing closely. Every Lovable number below comes from Lovable's own documentation, checked on **October 27, 2026**. We didn't run paid test projects, so you'll find Lovable's published prices and examples here, not our own benchmarks.

## What's on the Lovable Free plan

The Free plan is a real way to try Lovable, but it's tight:

- 5 daily build credits, capped at 30 a month. Lovable's own example: you get daily credits for the first 6 days of the month (6 × 5 = 30), then none until the 1st.
- A daily chat allowance, smaller than on paid plans.
- A 20-credit monthly Cloud grant, a 4-credit monthly AI grant and a 1-credit monthly connector grant.
- Unlimited workspace members, private projects, and Git sync with GitHub, GitLab and Bitbucket.
- Publishing to invited people only, up to 10 per project.

What Free leaves out: a custom domain, removing the Lovable badge, editing or downloading the code in Lovable, rollovers and top-ups. When you run out, you wait or you upgrade.

Students and teachers can get 50% off Pro with 100 credits a month, billed monthly, for up to 12 months. The discount only covers that one tier, and you have to verify while you're still on Free.

## How Lovable credits work

One balance pays for five kinds of usage: building, chatting, Cloud hosting and backend, AI features in your deployed app, and some managed connectors. Lovable spends the matching free grants first (the daily chat allowance, daily build credits, and the monthly Cloud, AI and connector grants), then your general credits, starting with whichever expire soonest.

### What a change costs

Plan mode costs 1 credit per message, plus any subagent research Lovable runs while planning. Build mode is usage-based: the bigger the request, the more it costs. Lovable publishes these illustrative examples:

| Prompt | Credits used |
|--------|--------------|
| Make the button gray | 0.50 |
| Remove the footer | 0.90 |
| Add authentication | 1.20 |
| Build a landing page with images | 2.00 |

Lovable notes that these are examples and that actual costs vary with your project. As rough arithmetic, 100 Pro credits would cover about 200 edits the size of "make the button gray", or about 50 landing pages with images. Real projects mix small and large changes, plus the fixes in between.

A few other rules worth knowing:

- **Chat** is priced per message, "typically a fraction of a credit." It uses the daily chat allowance first, then daily build credits, then general credits.
- **Goal runs** (the `/goal` command) bill on the work done but cost "noticeably more," because Lovable keeps working until the goal is met.
- **Long messages** can run for up to 10 hours. Lovable checks in each time a single message crosses your check-in level, 20 credits by default. That check-in is a notification, not a hard cap, and the feature is in beta.
- **Stopped requests** are charged for the work completed. If you stop before Lovable changes anything, you aren't charged.

### Cloud and AI usage after launch

Your bill doesn't stop when you finish building. If your app uses Lovable's built-in backend, the database server, storage, network, compute and realtime traffic all draw credits. AI features inside your app are billed per model call, based on the model, tokens, images or seconds of video and voice. The monthly Cloud grant (20 credits) and AI grant (4 credits) cover the first part of that; after that, it comes out of your general credits.

Lovable's docs don't list a per-unit rate for Cloud usage, so you can't price a busy app in advance from the docs alone. If your balance hits zero, building stops, AI features in deployed apps stop working, and apps on the built-in backend pause. Your published site stays live.

### Rollover, expiry and top-ups

- Unused monthly credits roll over while your subscription is active, but they still expire 2 months after they were issued on monthly plans, or 1 month after the annual period ends on annual plans.
- Daily credits reset at 00:00 UTC and never roll over.
- Top-ups cost $15 per 50 credits on Pro ($0.30 per credit) and $30 per 50 credits on Business ($0.60 per credit). They're valid for 12 months.

Compare that with the plan rate: Pro's 100-credit tier works out to $0.25 per credit, and Lovable's docs use that same $0.25 rate for Pro and $0.50 for Business. Topping up regularly costs more than moving up a tier, and Lovable says so itself.

## What changes after October 31, 2026

Lovable's credits documentation says the current chat pricing model, "including the per-message price and the daily chat allowance, applies through October 31, 2026." It also calls the monthly Cloud, AI and connector grants "a temporary offering and subject to change."

Build mode, Plan mode, image and video generation, and Cloud and AI usage follow standard pricing and aren't part of that offer. So the price of building isn't what's flagged to change. What may change is how much chat you get for free, and whether the monthly hosting and AI grants stay. If your workflow leans on chat, budget as if the free allowance could shrink.

## Who Lovable's pricing fits

- **Good fit:** a founder or maker who wants to start free, build a polished web app, and keep hosting, data and AI features inside one platform and one bill.
- **Good fit:** teams that need SSO and governance and will pay Business rates for them.
- **Harder fit:** heavy iterators. Every change costs credits, and runtime usage shares the same balance, so a successful app can compete with your own building for credits.
- **Harder fit:** anyone who needs a mobile app or slide deck next to the web app, or who wants to hand a client their own domain and code on the cheapest plan.

## FAQ

**How much does Lovable cost?**
Lovable has a Free plan. Pro starts at $25 a month and Business at $50 a month, each for 100 credits, and both scale up to 10,000 credits a month ($2,250 on Pro, $4,300 on Business, billed monthly). Annual billing gives two months free. Enterprise is priced by contract.

**How do Lovable credits work?**
One credit balance pays for building, chat, Cloud hosting, AI features in your app and some connectors. Plan mode costs 1 credit per message; Build mode costs depend on the request, with Lovable's examples ranging from 0.50 credits for a button color change to 2.00 for a landing page with images.

**Is Lovable free?**
Yes, there's a Free plan with 5 build credits a day, up to 30 a month, plus a daily chat allowance and small monthly Cloud and AI grants. It doesn't include a custom domain, code download or top-ups.

**What happens to Lovable pricing after October 31, 2026?**
Lovable's docs say the current chat pricing and daily chat allowance apply through October 31, 2026, and that the monthly Cloud, AI and connector grants are temporary. Build and Plan mode pricing isn't part of that offer. Check Lovable's docs for what replaces it.

**Is Lovable worth it?**
For a web app you want to build and host in one place, with a free plan to start, it can be. If you need mobile apps or slides too, want the backend in your own accounts, or build for clients, Youmake is the better fit. [Start a 3-day Youmake trial for $1](https://www.youmake.dev/).
