## What does "white label" mean for a website builder?

The term gets used loosely. For agency work it breaks into four separate questions:

| Question | Why it matters to a client |
|---|---|
| Does the site live on the client's own domain? | The client sees `theirbusiness.com`, not a builder subdomain. |
| Can you remove or avoid the tool's branding on the live site? | Visitors should not see a vendor's badge. |
| Can the client leave with their site? | Code export or GitHub sync means no lock-in. |
| Can you hand over the account or the project? | Hand-off is part of a professional delivery. |

A builder can answer "yes" to the first and "no" to the third. Check each one before you promise it to a client. The lock-in question is covered in depth in [AI app builders that let you export your code](/articles/ai-app-builder-code-export).

## A delivery checklist for client sites

- **Brief approved in writing** before the first prompt.
- **Facts verified.** Addresses, hours, prices and licenses come from the client, not from the model.
- **Mobile checked** on a real phone.
- **Forms tested** end to end, including where the submissions land.
- **Security scan run** and results reviewed before publishing.
- **Domain and DNS** set up in the client's name, with the registrar login stored for them.
- **Hand-off note** that says where the code lives, who can edit, and how to ask for changes.

For ongoing client work that needs logins and shared files, pair the site with a portal. ## How should an agency price AI-built sites?

Price the outcome, not the hours the builder saved you. A client is buying a site that brings in inquiries, plus someone who answers when it breaks. Many agencies split the work into a fixed build fee and a monthly care fee for updates and monitoring.

## Honest limits

- **Review is your job.** AI drafts can contain wrong claims or odd layouts. Read every page.
- **Complex integrations need care.** Custom booking, memberships or heavy e-commerce deserve a test plan and sometimes a developer.
- **Ownership terms belong in your contract.** Decide who holds the domain, hosting and code, and write it down.
- **Compliance is yours.** Privacy policies, cookie rules and accessibility requirements depend on the client's region and industry.

## FAQ

**What is a white label AI website builder?**
It is a builder that lets you deliver sites under your own brand and the client's own domain, without the tool's name front and center. Check domain, branding, export and hand-off separately.

**Can I resell sites built with Youmake?**
You can build sites for clients and publish them to the client's domain. The Agency plan and team seats are designed for client work. Confirm the current terms on the Youmake site before you build your pricing around them.

**Will my client own the code?**
With code export or GitHub sync, a client can take the project to another developer. Agree on this in your contract at the start.
